# The Constitution of the Republic of the Closet

## Preamble

We, the citizens, wayfarers, and temporary residents of the Closet Republic, in recognition that forced identities are an offense against human dignity, declare our autonomy. We establish this sanctuary not as an exile, but as an intentional territory where the right to self-definition is absolute, immutable, and prior to the sanction of any external state, bureaucracy, or family council.

---

## Part 1: The Constitution

1. Closet Republic or CR is an organization of members as defined in Part 3.
2. The Governing Body of Closet republic or GCR or Government of Closet Republic as formed by Part 4 is responsible for the maintenance of CR.
3. Only GCR has authority to write into this constitution of CR.
4. A section can only be written into if the said section does not conflict with the preceding sections.
5. A section can only be added into part 1 if and only if the 95% of GCR supports it.
6. A section can only be added to part 2, part 3, part 4 if and only if at least 75% of GCR supports it.
7. A section can only be added to part 5 if and only if at least 50% of GCR supports it.
8. Once a section is added into part 1, part 2, part 3.1 it cannot be modified in any manner.
9. GCR and members of CR are required to respect the rights of fellow humans as mentioned in Part 2.

---

## Part 2: Fundamental Human Rights

1. Everyone has the right to live peacefully.
2. Everyone has freedom of speech, expression and self-identity.
3. Everyone has the right to protest peacefully.
4. Everyone has the right to form associations or unions.
5. Only the individual has the ultimate authority and autonomy over their body and in no case should anyone be subjected to forced identity and expression, torture in any form, forced labour, slavery, forced intimate contact of any manner.
6. Everyone has the right to be treated equally.

---

## Part 3: Members of Closet Republic

1. Only those who have not violated and have agreed to uphold Fundamental Human Rights as mentioned in Part 2 can be members of the Closet Republic.

---

## Part 4: Governance of Closet Republic

1. The GCR consists of founders of CR and elected representatives from the members of Closet Republic.
